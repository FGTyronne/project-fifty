## Interpretation

M8 materially improved transaction economics relative to the earlier intraday strategy family, but the remaining stock-selection/rotation logic did not demonstrate durable out-of-sample edge. The holdout loss was modest rather than catastrophic, yet the negative median walk-forward result and 13.92% trade rate fail the criteria that were fixed before the holdout was exposed.

The v5 strategy must not be tuned against this disclosed M8 period.

The next experiment, if continued, should remove cross-sectional stock selection entirely and test whether Project Fifty can first establish a robust, very-low-turnover single broad-market exposure/cash baseline on a new disjoint historical period. That would isolate market-timing/risk-budget value from stock-picking noise and provide a much cleaner baseline for a GBP 50-scale account.
