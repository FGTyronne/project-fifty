## Interpretation

Compared with M5, modeled costs fell sharply, turnover fell materially, walk-forward performance crossed both robustness thresholds, and drawdown remained small. The remaining failure is narrow but real: the holdout lost 0.11% and the trade rate of 23.82% still exceeded the predeclared 21.33% ceiling.

The project must not weaken either gate after observing this result. Autonomous paper execution remains blocked.

The most plausible remaining structural source of churn is regime-budget oscillation. The v3 strategy still changes desired gross exposure as the raw regime detector moves between risk-on, neutral and risk-off, which can trigger portfolio-wide partial rebalances even when the selected holdings themselves have not changed.
