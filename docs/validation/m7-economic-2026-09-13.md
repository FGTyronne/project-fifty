## Interpretation

The isolated regime-persistence hypothesis did not improve the strategy. The v4 strategy lost 6.49% on the frozen holdout while SPY gained 7.04%, trade rate increased relative to M6, and walk-forward robustness deteriorated. This is evidence against continuing to tune the same intraday target-weight/regime architecture with additional persistence parameters.

The result does not justify changing the M7 gates after observation. Autonomous paper execution remains blocked.

## Next research direction

The next milestone should be an architectural reset rather than another confirmation-count adjustment. Project Fifty should evaluate a deliberately low-frequency, daily-data strategy whose holding periods and rebalance frequency are naturally compatible with a tiny capital base. The purpose is to determine whether the existing signal family has any robust economic value when transaction frequency is structurally constrained, while continuing to benchmark honestly against cash and passive SPY.

M7's disclosed dataset must not be reused as an untouched holdout for the next strategy version.
