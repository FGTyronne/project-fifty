## Interpretation

M5 materially improved the behaviour of the strategy versus the M4 baseline. The frozen holdout was profitable, drawdown was modest and it beat SPY over that same period. However, the system still trades too frequently and the walk-forward evidence is not sufficiently robust. The remaining failure mode is routine exit/rotation churn rather than market-data connectivity or catastrophic loss behaviour.

Project Fifty therefore remains blocked from autonomous paper execution.

## Next milestone

M6 must focus on exit persistence and churn suppression. It should distinguish immediate risk-reducing exits from ordinary signal exits, require persistence/confirmation before routine position removal or replacement, preserve immediate SHOCK/constitutional exits, and revalidate on a disjoint dataset. The M5 holdout disclosed in this document must not be used as a fresh holdout for M6 tuning.
